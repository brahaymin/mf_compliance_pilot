What happens on December 11, 2026

The SEC's amended Names Rule (Rule 35d-1, adopted September 2023) says a fund whose name suggests a focus, such as a type of investment, an industry, or a region, must adopt a policy to invest at least 80% of its assets in line with that name, and must define the name's terms in its prospectus.

In March 2025 the SEC extended the compliance dates: June 11, 2026 for fund groups with $1 billion or more in net assets, and December 11, 2026 for smaller fund groups (less than $1 billion in net assets at the end of their most recent fiscal year). An existing fund generally comes into compliance with its first annual prospectus update or annual report filed on or after that date. The SEC separately extended the date for the rule's Form N-PORT reporting changes in 2026.
