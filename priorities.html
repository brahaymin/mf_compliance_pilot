Feature guide

What the live sample already does

The public pilot at this site is a static, privacy-screened browser of structured sales-charge waiver, rights of accumulation, letter of intent, breakpoint, and waiver-limitation language from official SEC filings. It is a partner demo, not the complete live library.

  • Search and filters. Reviewers can find a ticker, fund family, intermediary, or keyword, then narrow by category, current vs historical status, waiver type, and named intermediary. This is the start of a prospectus-first look-back: begin with the offering document, not informal knowledge.
  • Current vs historical badges. Every rule is marked current (green) or historical (red), with filing date, the gap since the prior version, and any later 497 amendment. That prevents someone from applying superseded waiver text to a later trade date.
  • Version timeline. Opening the chain shows how the same rule changed across filings, which supports “what language governed this date?” reviews.
  • Official filing link. Each card cites the Accession Number and a link to the public SEC filing, so a reviewer can walk from a ticker to the governing prospectus excerpt.
  • Legacy snippets tab. An older regex-extract sample remains available for comparison; the structured sample is the default.

Other ready-now features, and what still has to wait

The remaining ready-now ideas on the priorities list — including breakpoint completeness by Series ID, the $25,000–$1 million ladder chart, householding and related-party phrase banks, retirement and charity evidence for the 2011–2015 waiver-sweep window, R-share vs Class A sibling checks, 529 language mismatches, Rule 2341 schedule quality checks, and identifier completeness — can be built from tables already in the library. Several of those need Series ID or the raw breakpoint tier JSON, which are in the database but not in this public sample, so they are described on the cards rather than illustrated here.

The thirteen “Awaiting data” ideas need something the live tables do not yet have: a local fee-table or prospectus HTML parse (no new SEC download), weekly official ticker-file snapshots, official Series/Class Active–Inactive–Merged status, Form N-CEN, or the EDGAR submissions index. Those should wait until the historical download finishes.
